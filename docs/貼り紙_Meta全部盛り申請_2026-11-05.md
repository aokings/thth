## 1. 共通の前置き（各権限・機能の説明の先頭に貼る・計画 2-6・2-7）

```
THTH is an open-source command-line tool for researching, publishing and managing posts on a person's own social media accounts: Threads, Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts and Meta ad accounts, as well as Bluesky, Mastodon and X. People usually drive it through their own AI assistant (for example Claude Code): the assistant runs the same commands a person would type. The operator (gotoq) runs the server side: people connect their own account on Meta's authorization screen (Threads, or Facebook Login for Business for Pages, Instagram and ad accounts) and receive a personal key; they do not run a server or a Meta app of their own. With that key, the `thth` command on their own computer acts on their own account through the operator's server. THTH acts only when the account's owner (or the owner's assistant) asks for it, and never posts, replies, deletes, sends messages or starts ads on its own. Because an assistant can make mistakes, every account has safety limits that the owner controls: a minimum interval between posts, daily caps on posts and deletions, quiet hours, and an automatic stop when too many actions happen within ten minutes; everything that spends money or cannot be undone (ads delivery, deletions, messages, live video, call-to-action buttons) needs a two-step confirmation by a person. An assistant can only tighten these limits; loosening them, resuming a stopped account and issuing or revoking the key are done by the owner on https://thth.me/activity with their account secret, where they also see everything that was published or deleted. THTH itself sends no data to AI providers; what a command shows is passed to the owner's assistant only when they use one, and only to the AI provider the owner chose for that assistant, at the owner's own instruction. Access tokens reach the operator's server through the authorization flow, are kept in owner-only files, are redacted from logs and output, and are never returned to the command or to assistants; the key is shown once and only its hash is kept. When THTH refuses an action, it says why, how to make it pass and what would work better. Privacy policy: https://thth.me/privacy/ — Terms: https://thth.me/terms/
```

訳: THTH は、自分の SNS アカウント（Threads・Facebook ページ・Instagram のプロ口座・Meta の広告アカウント、ほかに Bluesky・Mastodon・X）で調べる・投稿する・管理するためのオープンソースのコマンドラインツールです。多くの人は自分の AI アシスタント（例 Claude Code）を通して使い、アシスタントは人が打つのと同じ命令を打ちます。運営者（gotoq）がサーバ側を動かします。利用者は Meta の認可画面（Threads か、ページ・IG・広告の Facebook ログイン for Business）で自分の口座を接続して個人の鍵を受け取り、自分のサーバや Meta アプリは持ちません。その鍵で、自分の機械の `thth` 命令が運営者のサーバを通して自分の口座を動かします。THTH が動くのは持ち主（か持ち主のアシスタント）が求めたときだけで、自分から投稿・返信・削除・メッセージの送信・広告の開始をしません。どの口座にも持ち主が決める安全装置（最短間隔・1 日の上限・夜間・急な連投の自動停止）があり、お金が動くもの・戻せないもの（広告の配信・削除・メッセージ・ライブ・行動ボタン）は人の二段確認が要ります。アシスタントは安全装置を締めることしかできず、緩める・戻す・鍵の発行と取り消しは持ち主が https://thth.me/activity で行います。THTH 自身は AI の提供者にデータを送りません。命令が見せたものは、持ち主がアシスタントを使うときだけ、持ち主が自分で選んだ提供者に、持ち主の指示で渡ります。トークンは認可の流れでサーバに届き、所有者だけが読めるファイルに置き、ログと出力では伏せ、命令にもアシスタントにも返しません。断るときは、なぜ・どうすれば通るか・どうすればより良いかを示します。

裏【読】: 安全装置 `thth/guard.py`、二段確認 `thth/approval.py`・`thth/meta_write.py`・`thth/ads/ops.py`、鍵 `thth/activity.py`、前向きな断り `docs/原則_前向きな断り.md`・`thth/refusals.py`。「利用者が自分で選んだ AI 提供者」の 1 文は計画 2-7（Platform Terms 3.c.ii.3）。**TypeSafe の規約の確認（masaru）は未**。

## 2. Threads・承認済み 10 権限（説明の差し替え・計画 2-1・2-4・2-5・2-6）

流れ A（新しい申請に入れられる）なら各欄に §1 ＋ 下の本文。流れ B（入れられない）なら §11 の文を「質問する」から送る。録画は 9/27 の `review0.mp4` が使える（差し替えの欄に動画が要るかは【masaru のログインで読む】）。

### 2.1 `threads_basic`

```
threads_basic lets THTH display the app user's own Threads posts and profile to that user. When the account is connected, THTH reads its user ID and username (/me) and keeps them with the account, so every later action is bound to that account; `thth doctor <account>` repeats this check and shows what the token can do. `thth posts <account>` lists the account's own recent posts (ID, link, time and text) so the owner can check what is live and choose a post to reply to or delete. Before a reply is sent, THTH may read the one post being answered to record a one-way key for its author; the text of that post is discarded.
```

訳: 持ち主自身の投稿とプロフィールを持ち主に見せる。接続時に /me のユーザー ID とユーザー名を読んで口座に結び、`thth doctor`・`thth posts` で使う。返信の前に答える先の 1 件を読んで投稿者の一方向の鍵だけ残し、本文は捨てる。

### 2.2 `threads_content_publish`

```
THTH creates and publishes content to the app user's own Threads profile when the owner asks for it: through their own AI assistant or the `thth` command (`thth send <account> --text …`, or a scheduled draft). The text, with its topic tag, reply target, place and attachments if any, is sent unchanged through the container-then-publish flow. Before publishing, THTH checks the account's safety limits (minimum interval, daily cap, quiet hours, burst stop) and refuses with the time when it may try again. Images have location and other embedded metadata removed before upload, and attachments reach Threads through short-lived links. The resulting post ID and link are recorded in the account's post history on the operator's server.
```

訳: 持ち主が求めたときだけ投稿する（`thth send`・予約）。本文は変えずに送り、送る前に安全装置を確かめる。画像の位置情報は外す。投稿 ID とリンクを記録する。

### 2.3 `threads_manage_replies`

```
THTH creates a reply on behalf of the app user's Threads profile when the owner asks for it: to replies under their own posts, to mentions of their account, or to a public post they chose to join. A reply names the post it answers (`thth send <account> --text … --reply-to <post_id>`) and counts toward the daily cap and the burst stop. THTH can also hide or unhide a reply under the owner's own post on request (`thth reply hide|unhide`). THTH never replies on its own and does not change who can reply to a post.
```

訳: 持ち主が求めたときだけ返信する（`--reply-to`）。自分の投稿の下の返信を隠す・戻すも求めたときだけ。自動返信はしない。

### 2.4 `threads_read_replies`

```
THTH gets replies to threads owned by the app user so they can see who answered and decide whether to reply. Its collection (`thth collect`) fetches the reply tree of each recent post at fixed ages after publishing, from 1 hour to 30 days, and saves each reply's username, text, time and link in the account's records on the operator's server. `thth replies <account>` shows these replies, and `thth unanswered <account>` lists the ones not yet answered. `thth thread <account> <post_id>` reads a branch on request and saves nothing. When the account's Threads webhook is subscribed, new replies also arrive through Meta's notifications held on thth.me for at most 7 days until the server collects them. The saved replies are kept until the account leaves THTH.
```

訳: 自分の投稿への返信を決まった刻みで採り、ユーザー名・本文・時刻・リンクを口座の記録に保存する。`thth thread` はその場で読むだけ。webhook を購読すれば通知でも届く（thth.me に最長 7 日）。退出まで持つ。

### 2.5 `threads_manage_insights`

```
THTH gets insights for the app user's Threads profile and for the individual threads it published, so the owner can learn which topics and forms work for them. Its collection reads each post's views, likes, replies, reposts, quotes and shares at fixed ages after publishing (1 hour, 6 hours, 1, 3, 7 and 30 days), and once a day reads the account's metrics for the previous day (views, likes, replies, reposts, quotes, followers and link clicks). The numbers are saved in the account's records on the operator's server and shown to the owner with `thth measured <account>`, `thth topics <account>` and the daily observation summary. If the owner chooses, figures computed from their own metrics can be posted on THTH's plaza, which by default is visible only within the same project.
```

訳: 自分の投稿と口座のインサイトを決まった刻みで読み、口座の記録に保存して `thth measured` などで見せる。

### 2.6 `threads_delete`

```
THTH deletes the app user's own Threads post only when the owner asks for it: through their own AI assistant or `thth retract <account> <post_id>` (the command first shows the post and a digest and deletes only when that digest is confirmed). Deletions have their own daily cap and count toward the burst stop, so a malfunctioning assistant cannot delete an account's history. THTH sends one DELETE request for that post ID and reports success only when Threads confirms it. The time, the person and the reason are added to the account's record of that post. THTH never deletes posts on its own.
```

訳: 求めたときだけ、二段確認のうえで 1 件消す。削除の上限と連投の止めが掛かる。記録は消さない。

### 2.7 `threads_keyword_search`（差し替え・計画 2-4 の (a)〜(e)）

```
THTH shows the app user public content that they search for with a specific keyword, so they can see what people are saying about a topic before posting or joining a conversation, and lets them publish a response to a public post they found. On request (`thth topics <account> --search <word>`, `thth where <account> <words>`), and in the daily observation summary using up to 5 keywords the operator sets for the account, it shows counts, the number of distinct authors, the share of the most frequent authors, the latest time and, for individual posts, the author, a link and the full text. These results are shown for that request only; their text and authors are not saved, and the run log keeps only the search terms and the number of results. Two kinds of numbers can be kept, both without post text, post IDs, usernames or links: (1) when the user asks for it with `thth where … --keep`, THTH saves per day and search term only counts computed from that search (how many results, how many contain a question mark, how many match each category of a word list the user supplies), with numbers below 5 saved as missing, readable only within that project through `thth where-log`; (2) the observation map, which is off by default and turned on by the operator, searches at most once a day for topic keywords a person registered for a project (at most 20; @handles, URLs and personal names are refused) and saves only daily figures per keyword for at most 180 days (number of posts, distinct authors, share of the three most frequent authors, a rounded age of the latest post and co-occurrence counts, with any number below 5 stored as missing), shown only within that project and deleted when the project leaves or on request. When the owner wants to join a conversation found this way, THTH can show that public post's thread on request (`thth thread <account> <post_id>`, read on the spot and not saved) and reads the one post being answered when a reply is prepared; any response the owner publishes is handled like a post (section on threads_manage_replies). When the owner works through an AI assistant, what these commands show is passed to the AI provider the owner chose for that assistant.
```

訳: 語で検索した公開の投稿を持ち主に見せ、見つけた投稿への返事を出せるようにする（Allowed Usage の言葉）。求めの検索（`topics --search`・`where`）と観測の一枚で、件数・投稿者の異なり数・占有率・最新の時刻と、個々の投稿の投稿者・リンク・**本文の全文**を見せ、保存しない。残す数は 2 つで、どちらも本文・ID・名前・リンクを含まない。(1) `where --keep` の語ごとの数（5 未満は欠測・`where-log` で読む）、(2) 観測の地図の日々の集計（最大 180 日）。枝はその場で読む。アシスタントを使うときは、持ち主が選んだ提供者に渡る。

裏【読】: 9/23 承認時の説明との差は、(a) `--keep` の件数（3.18.0）、(b) 本文の全文（10/15）、(c) 世間の層の 180 日（`THTH_MAP_WORLD`）、(d) `where-log` の当たり率、(e) 選んだ AI 提供者。privacy の「Keyword search」と同じ。

### 2.8 `threads_manage_mentions`

```
THTH shows the app user the public content in which their Threads account is mentioned, so they can see who is talking to them and decide whether to answer. Its collection (and, when subscribed, Meta's webhook notifications) saves each mention (username, text, time and link) in the account's records on the operator's server. `thth mentions <account>` shows current mentions on request and marks the ones already answered, and the daily observation summary lists the ones not yet answered. Any answer is a reply the owner publishes the same way as a post; THTH never replies automatically. Saved mentions are kept until the account leaves THTH.
```

訳: 自分への公開の言及を採って記録に保存し、`thth mentions` で見せる。答えは持ち主の返信。退出まで持つ。

### 2.9 `threads_profile_discovery`（計画 2-5・許可用途の言葉に寄せる）

```
THTH lets the app user find profiles of public Threads accounts and public posts of these accounts, so they can understand the accounts in their conversations (for example someone who replied to or mentioned them, or an account posting on the same topic) before deciding whether and how to engage. `thth profile <account> <username>` and `thth who <account> @username --profile` request only public fields: username, name, profile picture, biography, follower count, total likes, quotes, reposts and views, and verification status. The result is shown for that request and is not saved. THTH does not look up profiles on its own.
```

訳: 公開の Threads アカウントのプロフィールを、持ち主が求めたときに引いて見せる（Allowed Usage の言葉）。公開の項目だけ。保存しない。

### 2.10 `threads_location_tagging`

```
THTH helps the app user tag public locations to their posts. A draft may name a place: `thth location search <account> <name>` shows up to 5 candidate places (name, address, city, country and ID), and the owner writes the chosen place into the draft. The place name and ID are shown with the text before it is sent, and only that post carries the tag. THTH never infers or adds a location on its own, and location search results are not saved.
```

訳: 場所の検索で候補を見せ、持ち主が選んだ場所をその投稿にだけ付ける。推測しない。保存しない。

## 4. Facebook ページ（判断不要の 10 ＋ 判断 ①③ の 3）

招待は `--media facebook`（Facebook ログイン for Business・許可の画面で本人がページを選ぶ）。口座名の例は `PAGE`。

### 4.1 `pages_show_list`

```
THTH shows the app user the list of Pages they manage and verifies that they manage the Page the account is bound to. During authorization (Facebook Login for Business) the owner chooses the Page; THTH reads the Page's ID and name and keeps them with the account. `thth doctor <account>` repeats the check and shows the Page name. THTH acts only on Pages the owner chose.
```

訳: 認可で持ち主が選んだページの ID と名前を読み、口座に結ぶ。`thth doctor` で確かめる。

### 4.2 `business_management`（ページ・IG・広告で共通・1 つに数える）

```
THTH reads the business assets the app user grants on the Facebook Login for Business screen (Pages, Instagram professional accounts, ad accounts and product catalogs) so that the account can be bound to the one asset the owner chose. It does not claim assets, change business settings or add people. `thth doctor` and `thth ads doctor` show which assets the token can reach.
```

訳: 許可の画面で選んだ資産（ページ・IG・広告アカウント・カタログ）を読んで口座に結ぶ。資産の取得・設定の変更はしない。

### 4.3 `pages_manage_posts`

```
THTH publishes a post, photo or video to the app user's Page when the owner asks for it (`thth send <account> --text …`, with `--media` for photos, or a scheduled draft), updates the text of a post THTH published (`thth edit`, two-step confirmation) and deletes a post THTH published (`thth retract`, two-step confirmation). Safety limits apply as for Threads. The post ID and link are recorded in the account's post history; deletions keep the record with the time, the person and the reason.
```

訳: 求めたときだけページに投稿・直す（二段）・消す（二段）。安全装置が掛かる。記録は消さない。

### 4.4 `pages_read_engagement`

```
THTH gets content posted by the app user's Page and metadata about the Page: its collection (`thth collect`) reads the Page's own recent posts and their metrics at fixed ages after publishing, and once a day the Page's follower counts, and saves the numbers in the account's records. `thth posts <account>` lists the Page's own posts and `thth measured <account>` shows the numbers. THTH does not read the names or profile pictures of the Page's followers.
```

訳: 自分のページの投稿と数、フォロワー数を読んで記録に保存する。フォロワーの名前と画像は読まない。

### 4.5 `pages_read_user_content`

```
THTH gets user-generated content on the app user's Page: comments on the Page's posts, through its collection and through Meta's webhook notifications (`feed`) held on thth.me for at most 7 days until the server collects them. Each comment (the commenter's name, the text, the time and the link) is saved in the account's records so the owner can see who answered and decide whether to reply (`thth replies <account>`, `thth unanswered <account>`). THTH does not read ratings or posts the Page is tagged in with this permission, and does not delete other people's comments with it.
```

訳: 自分のページの投稿へのコメントを採集と webhook で読み、名前・本文・時刻・リンクを記録に保存する。評価と他人の投稿は読まない。

### 4.6 `pages_manage_engagement`

```
THTH publishes a comment on the app user's Page post when the owner asks for it (`thth send <account> --text … --reply-to <comment_id>`), and hides, unhides or deletes a comment under the Page's own post on request (`thth reply hide|unhide|delete`; delete needs a two-step confirmation). Comments count toward the daily cap and the burst stop. THTH never comments on its own and does not like posts with this permission.
```

訳: 求めたときだけコメントで返す・隠す・戻す・消す（消すのは二段）。自動ではしない。いいねはしない。

## 6. 広告（判断不要の 3 ＋ 判断 ②⑤ の 2）

訳: 自分の広告アカウントで、ページの投稿から PAUSED の広告を作り、始めるのは二段確認。止める・下げるは確認なし。上げる命令は無い。操作は記録する。

訳: リードのフォームの一覧と、フォームのリードを端末に出すだけ。保存しない。THTH から連絡はしない。

## 13. データの扱いの質問（聞かれたら・9/27 の §5 を今の実態に）

- 処理者: 運営者のサーバ（VM・日本）と Cloudflare（thth.me・Workers／Durable Objects／R2: 認可の中継・持ち主の画面・添付・削除の依頼・Meta の通知）。AI の提供者には送らない（利用者が自分のアシスタントを使うときだけ、利用者が選んだ提供者に、利用者の指示で渡る）。

- 削除: `thth account leave`・`https://thth.me/data-deletion`。
- 当局の要請: 合法性の審査・違法なら争う・最小限（9/27 の回答のまま）。
