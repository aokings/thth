THTH — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-12 · 日本語はこの下 · Terms of Service

Who runs THTH

THTH is an open-source command-line tool for publishing and managing posts on your own social media accounts; people usually drive it through their own AI assistant. The operator, gotoq, runs it on an operator-managed server (a virtual machine) for invited users and for the operator's own accounts. On that server THTH handles authorization, drafts, publishing, safety limits and records.

Invited users do not run their own server or register their own Meta app. Authorization starts in one of two ways: an invited user opens the invitation link the operator sent and presses the button on that page, or the operator starts it from the server with thth auth. Either way, the user reviews the account and permissions on the platform and approves. Afterwards, posts, replies and deletions happen only when the user asks for them, through any of THTH's tools (the command line, an AI assistant the user chose, or the owner's page); THTH does not act without the owner's permission, given each time or through rules the owner set. People who install the THTH software and run it themselves operate their own copy; this policy describes the operator's service.

Platforms and the data THTH uses

THTH works with Threads, Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts and Meta ad accounts, and with Bluesky, Mastodon and X. For each connected account it uses:

  • Threads: the account's user ID and username; its posts and their metrics (views, likes, replies, reposts, quotes, shares); daily account metrics (views, likes, replies, reposts, quotes, followers, link clicks); replies to its posts; mentions of the account; and, on request, sharing one post to the linked Instagram account as an Instagram story (thth send --share-to-instagram).
  • Bluesky: its posts and their metrics (likes, replies, reposts, quotes), replies to its posts, and mentions and quotes of the account.
  • Mastodon: its posts and their metrics (likes, replies, reposts), replies to its posts, and public mentions of the account.
  • X: publishing approved posts, deleting a post on request, reposts and likes on request, the account's own recent posts and their metrics (views, likes, replies, reposts, quotes, bookmarks; for posts made within the last 30 days, also link clicks, profile clicks, engagements and video playback), and replies to its posts; and, when the owner's session asks, a search of other people's recent public posts and the replies in one post's conversation (paid reads, shown for that request only: the text and authors are not saved).
  • Facebook Pages: the Page's ID and name; the posts THTH published and their metrics (Page insights: views, engagements, media views); daily follower counts; comments on the Page's posts and mentions of the Page; on request, hiding or unhiding a comment (thth reply hide), the Page's call-to-action button, live videos, the Messenger inbox, and, for the owner's brand work, the branded content a business can advertise and the creator marketplace (described below). When the owner authorizes with Meta, THTH reads the authorizing user's email address once, only shows it to the owner (and the AI assistant the owner chose) and does not store it, so that the owner can confirm where service notices go; THTH records only that the address could be read.
  • Instagram: for a professional account linked to a Page, the account's ID and username; its posts and their metrics (insights); daily reach and follower counts; comments on its posts; the ID of the Threads account linked to it (read once); on request, hashtag search, the events the account hosts, likes the owner gives (thth like, recorded with the media ID so they can be undone), the Direct inbox, that is, its direct messages, and, for the owner's brand work, branded content settings (the creators the brand approves for tagging, the use of a tagged post in partnership ads), the branded content a business can advertise and the creator marketplace, and, for the owner's shop, product tags (described below).
  • Ad accounts: the ad account's ID, name, currency, spend cap and amount spent; the status and insight numbers of its campaigns, ad sets and ads; on request, a boost of a Page post (created paused), lowering a budget, extending an ad set's end date or lifetime budget within the owner's limits after a two-step confirmation, the business's Audience Network numbers, its shop settings, its product catalogs and the Page's lead forms (described under Ads, catalogs and leads).
  • WhatsApp: the business account's ID, its phone numbers (IDs, display names and quality ratings) and message templates (names, statuses and languages); on request, a new message template, a message sent from an approved template, and the events (such as a purchase) that followed a conversation, reported to Meta for measurement (described under WhatsApp).

Meta's permissions for Instagram, product catalogs and Threads ads expire 90 days after the user was last active; THTH tells the operator before that and the owner authorizes again to extend them. Once a year the operator confirms, in Meta's Data Use Checkup, that each permission is used as described on this page.

Replies and mentions. Replies to the user's posts on Threads, Bluesky and Mastodon, and mentions of the user's account on Threads, Bluesky (including quotes) and Mastodon, are saved to that account's records, including the other person's username, the text, the time and the link. THTH keeps them to manage the conversation: to show which replies have been answered and to prepare the user's replies, which are sent the same way as posts. Comments on the owner's Facebook Page posts, mentions of the Page, and comments on the owner's Instagram media are saved the same way (the commenter's name, the text, the time and the link); they reach THTH through its collection and, for connected Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts and Threads accounts whose webhooks the owner subscribed, through the notifications Meta sends to thth.me.

Keyword search. Search (Threads threads_keyword_search, and search on Bluesky, Mastodon and X) runs on request: when the user's session asks for a search, or for the observation summary, which uses search terms the operator sets for the account. The only other search is the observation map below, when the operator turns it on. Results of a search on request are shown for that request only: counts, the number of distinct authors, the share of the most frequent authors, the latest time, and for individual posts the author, a link and the full text. The text and authors of search results are not saved. Run logs record at most the search terms and the number of results. To improve how it finds conversations, the owner's session may also keep, in the project's own notes, per search term only numbers: how many results it judged worth answering and how many it answered (never post text, post IDs or usernames; numbers below 5 are not kept). When the user's session asks for it with thth where … --keep, THTH itself also saves, per day, platform and search term (with the narrowing words the user gave), only numbers computed from that search: how many results it returned, how many end with or contain a question mark, how many match each category of a word list the user supplies, and how many contain the narrowing words. Numbers below 5, including zero, are saved as missing. Post text, post IDs, usernames, author keys, links and exact times are not saved. These numbers are kept with the observation map figures below and follow the same rules (private location outside Git, the same retention, deletion on exit and on request, readable only within that project, through thth where-log). Without --keep nothing from the search is saved.

Public reads on Instagram and Facebook. thth where on an Instagram account uses Meta's hashtag search and shows, per result, the media ID, link and public counts; it requests no caption, image or username. Meta allows at most 30 distinct hashtags in 7 days per account, so THTH keeps, in state/<account>/ig_hashtags.json, only each hashtag and the time it was first searched, for 7 days, and refuses the next hashtag beyond the limit. thth where on a Facebook Page uses Pages Search and reads, of the public content of the Pages found, only the name, ID, link and public counts (no posts or comments). These results are shown for that request only; the run log keeps the search words and the number of results.

Observation map (daily keyword aggregates). It is off unless the operator turns it on. Its keywords are topic words that a person registers for a project: the operator registers them, at most 20 per project. THTH never adds keywords taken from other people's posts, and refuses @handles, URLs and words that look like personal names. When it is on, THTH searches each keyword at most once a day on Threads, Bluesky and Mastodon, using the project's own connected account on that platform (the 25 most recent results, of which posts from the last 24 hours are counted). It counts the results in memory and saves only daily figures per keyword and platform: the number of posts, the number of distinct authors, the share of the three most frequent authors, the number of results with an author name, the time since the latest post rounded to one of four ranges (under 1 hour, 1–6 hours, 6–24 hours, 24 hours or more), and, for each pair of keywords, how many of one keyword's results also contain the other. A number below 5 (posts, distinct authors or a pair count) is saved as missing instead of its value (a search that returned no posts is recorded as zero). Post text, post IDs, usernames, author keys, links and exact times are not saved.

These figures are kept in a private location on the operator's server, outside Git, for at most 180 days: each collection run deletes older days one day at a time, and days past 180 days are not shown even before they are deleted. When the project's last account exits, its whole map is deleted; when other accounts in the project remain, the figures for the leaving account's platform are deleted. On request, the operator deletes the figures for the whole project or for a date range. The figures are shown only within that project: through thth map show, the map line of the observation summary, and the same tools over MCP, to sessions whose credential covers that project. They are not shown to other owners, on the shared plaza, or in totals across projects. When the user's LLM session calls these tools, the keywords and figures are passed to that session's LLM provider.

Profiles and places. Public profile lookup (Threads threads_profile_discovery) shows another account's public profile fields on request; the result is not saved. Location search (threads_location_tagging) shows candidate places; a place the user chooses is written in the draft and attached only to that post. On Instagram and Facebook, thth who … --profile shows another professional account's or Page's public counts (followers, media count); thth embed returns the embed HTML of a public post through Meta's oEmbed endpoint; thth upcoming shows the events the owner's Instagram account hosts. These are shown for that request and not saved.

To count how often the same person appears without keeping names in those records, some records use a one-way key derived from the platform and the username.

Direct messages (Messenger and Instagram Direct)

THTH reads a Page's Messenger inbox or an Instagram professional account's Direct inbox only when the owner (or the assistant the owner chose) asks for it. thth dm list shows the conversations without text, and thth dm read shows the latest messages of one conversation (the text, the other person's name and ID, the times) on the owner's terminal; with --profile on Facebook it also shows the other person's locale, time zone and gender. None of this is saved. A reply is written by the owner (or the owner's assistant) and sent with thth dm send only after a two-step confirmation; THTH does not send a message without the owner's permission, given each time or through rules the owner set. A reply is sent only within 24 hours of the other person's last message; after that, only a human agent's reply marked with Meta's human_agent tag, within 7 days of it. To decide this, THTH reads the time of the last message at the moment of sending and keeps no record of it. A private reply to a comment (thth dm send --comment) is sent within 7 days of the comment, one per comment. THTH sends utility or marketing messages only when the owner decided them with a two-step confirmation; getting the recipients' agreement to receive them is the owner's responsibility, as Meta's rules require. On the owner's request, and only after a two-step confirmation, THTH can also report the outcome of a conversation, such as a purchase or an inquiry, to Meta (Conversions API for Business Messaging) so that the owner's advertising can be measured and optimized: the kind of outcome, an optional amount and currency, and the other person's ID within the Page or account. What THTH keeps is one run-log line per command: the command name, the platform, the permission used and the result (for a reported outcome, also its kind), with no text, name, ID or amount; it also keeps the ID of the Meta dataset that receives the outcomes and, for a short time after each report, a keyed one-way hash that tells whether the same outcome was just reported, because Meta does not remove duplicates and a second report would be counted twice.

Live video and call-to-action button

thth live create creates a live video on the owner's Page after a two-step confirmation and shows the streaming destination once on the owner's terminal; it is not saved. The owner streams with their own software, and thth live end ends the broadcast after a two-step confirmation. THTH does not stream, record or read the video. thth page cta show reads the Page's call-to-action button and thth page cta delete removes it after a two-step confirmation; nothing about the button is saved.

Branded content and the creator marketplace

When the owner works with creators, THTH can, at the owner's request, read and change the branded content settings of the owner's Instagram account: the list of creators the brand approves for tagging (the creators' account IDs), and, from the creator's side, whether a brand may use a post that tags it in partnership ads. Changes are made only after a two-step confirmation. THTH also reads the partnership content a business can advertise (post IDs and types, not the text) and searches the Instagram and Facebook creator marketplace for creators (their IDs). These answers are only shown to the owner (and the AI assistant the owner chose); THTH does not save them. What THTH keeps in its run log is only the command name, the platform, the permission used and the result.

Shop product tags

When the owner sells through an Instagram Shop, THTH can, at the owner's request, check whether the Instagram account can tag products, read the catalogs and products of that shop (IDs, names and review status) and the product tags on a post, and add a product tag to an existing post of the owner's account after a two-step confirmation. These answers are only shown to the owner (and the AI assistant the owner chose); THTH does not save them. What THTH keeps in its run log is only the command name, the platform, the permission used and the result.

WhatsApp

For a connected WhatsApp Business Account THTH keeps a ledger on the operator's server (waba/: the account's ID, the project and a daily limit of messages) and a state directory (waba_state/<name>/: the sign-in state, a stop mark and run logs). At the owner's request THTH reads the account's phone numbers and message templates and only shows them to the owner (and the AI assistant the owner chose); THTH does not save them. With the owner's confirmation it can submit a new message template to Meta for review, or send one message built from an approved template to the phone number the owner gives; getting that person's agreement to receive it is the owner's responsibility. The recipient's number and the text of a template or message are used for that request only and THTH does not save them. What THTH keeps in its run log is only the command name, the platform, the permission used and the result.

Ads, catalogs and leads

For a connected Meta ad account THTH keeps a ledger on the operator's server (adaccounts/: the ad account ID, the project, the Pages and Instagram accounts it may advertise for, and the spending limits the owner set) and a state directory (adaccount_state/<name>/): a daily snapshot of what THTH read (the account's name, currency, spend cap and amount spent, and the status and insight numbers of campaigns, ad sets and ads), one record per operation THTH carried out (the operation, the IDs and the result), the monthly budget estimate, and Marketing API call counts (meta_calls.json: per-day counts for the last 16 days of calls and errors, and the time and success of each of the last 500 calls, no content). Everything THTH creates (thth ads boost, from a post the owner's Page published, with the budget the owner gave) is paused; delivery starts only after the owner's two-step confirmation, and THTH raises a budget only after the owner's two-step confirmation. THTH's budget estimate is not an invoice; Meta's billing applies. thth ads catalog shows the business's product catalogs and products, thth ads leads shows lead forms and, on request, the leads of a form (the fields the person entered) on the owner's terminal, and thth ads network and thth ads shop show Audience Network figures and shop settings; none of these results are saved.

Google Ads and Search Console (read-only)

thth gads and thth gsc read the user's own Google Ads accounts (the account list, keyword ideas with their search volume, competition and bid ranges, and campaign figures) and Search Console properties (the property list, search clicks, impressions, click-through rate and position, and URL inspection results). They ask Google only for the read scopes adwords and webmasters.readonly, through the Google sign-in the user approves in their own browser. They run only on the user's own computer: the refresh token is kept in a file only the user can read (~/.config/thth/, mode 600), and nothing from Google is sent to the operator's server, to thth.me or to any other party. Results are shown on the user's terminal (or as JSON for the user's own tools) and are not saved by THTH. These tools only read; if THTH changes Google Ads, it does so only after the owner's two-step confirmation, and Google bills the owner directly. The user can revoke access at any time in their Google Account (Security, third-party access) and delete the token file. THTH's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Online shops (read-only)

thth shop can read the owner's own online shop on BASE, Shopify or STORES so that THTH can draft a post when an item is new, back in stock or reduced in price. It runs only on the owner's own computer: the owner makes a key for the shop (described below for each shop) and gives it to THTH, which keeps it in a file only the owner can read (~/.config/thth/, mode 600); THTH has no path that sends it to the operator's server or thth.me. THTH does not read orders or anything about the shop's customers, and it never writes to the shop. THTH does not follow redirects from the shop. It keeps one latest snapshot of the items on the owner's computer (ID, name, price, for Shopify and STORES the price of each variant with the variant's ID, stock or that the shop does not count it, whether on sale, image URL, the item page's URL when the shop gives one, and the time it changed); for items that are not public it keeps only the ID and that they are not public. Item descriptions are not kept. Drafts are written unapproved to the owner's queue, which is part of the owner's repository, and only for items that are public and can be bought; a draft holds the item's name, price, for Shopify and STORES the price of each variant with its ID (at most 50 variants), the item page's URL when the shop gives one (otherwise the shop's URL) and when THTH read it, and like any other draft it travels with the repository to the machine that posts it. When the approval is confirmed on a computer that holds the shop's token, THTH reads the item once more and refuses if the price, visibility or availability has changed; if variants were only added or removed, it says so and lets the approval through, except for a price-drop draft whose variants were removed; on a computer without the token it does not read again, says so, and lets the approval through. THTH does not read the item again at posting time. On the owner's computer THTH keeps the shop's ledger (its name, kind, project, for Shopify the shop's myshopify name, for STORES the shop page's URL that the owner entered, and where the token is) and a record of who added the shop, set the token, drafted and removed it, without the token. thth shop remove deletes the shop's ledger, snapshot and tokens on the computer where it runs. When the project's last account leaves, thth leave deletes the shop files on the computer where it runs; files on the owner's own computer are deleted there with thth shop remove.

BASE. The owner creates a personal access token on BASE with the read scopes items.read and user.read. THTH sends only GET requests to apiv2.thebase.com, to two paths: the shop (of which THTH keeps only the shop's ID, name, URL and whether it is open, and discards the rest, such as the login email address) and the item list. THTH keeps the token's expiry date, from which thth morning on that computer can warn 14 days ahead. The token on BASE itself is revoked on BASE. THTH is not provided by BASE.

Shopify. The owner creates an app in the Shopify Dev Dashboard with the read scope read_products, installs it on the shop and gives THTH the app's client ID and client secret (or the access token of a custom app the shop already has). THTH sends requests only to the owner's own shop at <shop>.myshopify.com, of two kinds: a request for an access token, and 2 fixed queries that read the shop's name and the products (including each variant's ID and price). When Shopify answers that a query costs too much or that the shop is busy, THTH sends the same fixed query again a few times, with a smaller page or after waiting (at most 4 times and 60 seconds of waiting in one read; past that, THTH stops the read and says so). They are POST requests because Shopify's Admin GraphQL API works that way, but they do not change the shop. THTH keeps the access token it obtains (valid for about 24 hours) in a separate file in the same place (~/.config/thth/, mode 600) and reuses it until shortly before it expires. Of the shop, THTH keeps only its myshopify domain, name and URL. THTH reads the names of the permissions the key has been granted and tells the owner when there are more than read_products; it does not keep that list, and the record keeps only how many extra permissions there were. THTH sends nothing to the item page and image URLs that Shopify returns; drafts carry the item page's URL but not image URLs. The key on Shopify itself is revoked in the Dev Dashboard, by removing the app from the shop or creating a new secret. THTH is not provided by Shopify.

STORES. The owner applies to STORES for a private app and gives THTH the credential that STORES issues for the shop. THTH sends only GET requests to api.stores.dev, to two paths: the shop (of which THTH keeps only the shop's ID and name) and the item list. THTH cannot choose what the credential is allowed to do. Whatever it allows, THTH calls only those two paths, so it does not read orders or payouts. The STORES API does not return the shop's URL, so the owner enters the shop page's URL when adding the shop; THTH writes it into drafts and sends nothing to it. If the owner gives the credential's expiry date, THTH keeps it, and thth morning on that computer can warn 14 days ahead. THTH cannot revoke the credential on STORES itself; the owner arranges that with STORES. THTH is not provided by STORES.

Blog articles on WordPress

THTH can publish an article from the owner's repository to the owner's self-hosted WordPress site after a person approves it. The owner creates an application password in WordPress for the user that writes the articles and enters it into THTH on the computer that sends them (the operator's server for accounts that run there, or the owner's own computer). THTH keeps it in an owner-only file (by default ~/.config/thth/, mode 600) on that computer and uses it to authenticate requests to that site. THTH sends requests to the site's own address over HTTPS (plain HTTP is accepted only for a test site on the same computer). Before connecting, THTH looks up the site's name and connects only to an address it found that is on the public internet, not to a loopback, private or link-local address. It uses four REST paths: posts (to create a post as published or as a draft, to update a post THTH created, and to read it back), categories and tags (to look up the names written in the draft), and users/me (to check which user the password belongs to and its roles). Each request carries the WordPress user name and the application password, and a User-Agent with the THTH version and a contact URL. The article THTH sends is the title, the body converted to HTML, the categories and tags, and whether to create it as published or as a draft. Before updating a post, THTH reads its current title, body and categories and compares them with what it last saw; if the post was changed elsewhere, THTH stops and reports instead of overwriting it. When the result of a send is unknown, THTH reads the 20 most recent posts that the user can see, drafts and private posts included, and shows on the terminal those whose title and time match. From users/me THTH keeps the user name and its roles in the account's check record. On the computer that sends, THTH keeps the approved body, the exact HTML it sent, the title, categories, the post's ID and URLs, hashes of what the site returned, the history of each delivery, run logs, and a record of who entered or removed the application password, without the password. It writes the post's ID, URL and time back to the draft file in the owner's repository. thth principal auth <name> --forget deletes the application password file on the computer where it runs; the application password itself is revoked in WordPress, under the user's profile. Published articles stay on the WordPress site until the owner removes them there. THTH is not provided by WordPress.

Attachments

Images, video and audio attached to a draft are checked before use. For images, THTH removes location data and other embedded metadata (XMP, IPTC, MakerNote, thumbnails, comments) without re-encoding. Video and audio are not altered; a file that carries location metadata, or metadata THTH cannot inspect, is refused. The checked file is stored in the account's records.

Files uploaded by invited users and files handed to a platform pass through Cloudflare R2 on thth.me. Each is reachable only through an unguessable, short-lived capability URL: 10 minutes for uploads and for images handed to a platform, 30 minutes for video handed to a platform, and one hour after publication is confirmed. The Worker deletes each stored object 24 hours after it was created, retrying up to 10 times; the storage bucket is also set to delete these objects after 24 hours.

Safety limits and the owner's activity page

Every account has safety limits that its owner controls: a minimum interval between posts, daily caps on posts and on deletions, quiet hours, an optional hold before an immediate post goes out, and an automatic stop when too many posts and deletions happen within a few minutes. The server enforces them from the account's own records and keeps a stop record (reason and time) on the operator's server. An AI assistant can only tighten these limits.

The owner's activity page (https://thth.me/activity) is opened with the account's username and account secret. A person record on thth.me holds a salted PBKDF2-SHA256 verifier with 100,000 iterations, generation and failure count, not the secret itself; the secret is transmitted only when the person submits a form and is not retained. Signing in sets a cookie limited to /activity that lasts 10 minutes. The page shows a summary that the server pushes every 10 seconds: what was published, deleted, scheduled or held (the first 60 characters of each text and the time), whether the account is stopped and why, today's counts against the limits, and the identifier and expiry of the assistant key. thth.me forgets the summary one hour after the last push. From the page the owner can stop the account, resume it, cancel a scheduled post, change the safety limits, and issue or revoke the assistant key; each of these actions is confirmed with the secret, carried out by the server within seconds, and kept on thth.me for at most one hour. THTH does not record who views the page; the Worker only applies a rate limit.

Assistant key. The key an owner gives to their own CLI or AI assistant is generated on thth.me, shown once to the owner, and never stored in clear: thth.me and the operator's server keep only its SHA-256 hash, its scope (that account) and its expiry (365 days). Issuing a new key revokes the previous one. Every post, deletion and schedule made with a key is recorded with the key's identifier. When the key is issued through the browser sign-in for the thth command (https://thth.me/login/…), thth.me holds the new key for the command to collect, for at most 10 minutes, and deletes it as soon as it is collected.

The command path (thth.me/api/v1). The thth command on the owner's own computer, and an AI assistant that runs it, act on the account through https://thth.me/api/v1 with the assistant key. thth.me checks the key's hash and rate limits (60 requests per minute per key, 120 per minute per address), holds the request for the operator's server, which fetches it and carries it out, and returns the result. thth.me keeps a request for at most 120 seconds and its result for at most 10 minutes, does not log request or result contents, and holds only the hash, account and expiry of each key. The operator's server verifies the key again before acting. Direct message text and names and a live video's streaming destination pass this path only within those limits; the operator's server does not write them to its request records or logs. Requests for a Meta ad account, including reading lead form answers, use the same path; the operator's server does not write lead answers, form names, product details or audience targeting to its request records or logs. An account that is read-only on this path (for example a Meta ad account before the operator sets limits) cannot post, send or create, but can still read that account's direct messages and lead form answers.

Authorization relay

The relay uses a Cloudflare Worker and Durable Object. It temporarily holds a registered authorization code for at most 300 seconds after receipt and within the 600-second session lifetime, and releases it once to the initiating server. Long-lived tokens, client secrets and PKCE verifiers are not sent to the relay. The application deletes the code when consumed or expired. Unregistered flows can use the existing return-URL paste fallback.

Reports

An invited user's session can file bug reports and requests. A report is stored in a private location on the operator's server with the account, project, title, text, reproduction steps and replies. A report containing something that looks like a secret (for example a token) is refused rather than stored. The change log records that a report was filed, answered or closed, without its text.

Plaza

The plaza (thth plaza, and the same tools over MCP) lets a user's sessions post measures they tried, findings and questions, with numbers, and reply to each other. Posts and replies are stored in a private location on the operator's server (owner-only files), not in the account's Git repository. A post that looks like it contains a secret is refused. Numbers shown as observations are computed by THTH from the account's own posts and metrics.

Who can read a post is chosen per post: project (the accounts of the same project), owner (the projects of an owner group that the operator registers; they read the original) or open (other owners who have joined the plaza). The default is project, or owner when the operator has registered the project in an owner group; open is never the default. Joining the open plaza is per project and off by default, and an open post is visible only when both the posting and the reading project have joined. The operator can read every post.

Making a post open is a two-step confirmation from the command line only: the first step shows exactly what other owners will see, and the post is placed only when the same content is confirmed. Other owners read a copy from which THTH has removed other people's information: text of other people's replies found in the account's records, their usernames, @handles other than the author's own, one-way author keys, and links to other people's social media posts. For the author's own posts the copy shows the first 60 characters and the link. Other owners see the project name, not the account name or the person who posted. If THTH cannot read the account's records, it does not make the copy.

The operator can hide a post with a reason (its author still sees it) and can remove a project from the open plaza. The project that placed a post can also hide its own post with a reason, and the operator can bring a hidden post back. THTH also records which plaza posts a session has read (the post ID and time only). When an account exits, its plaza posts and replies are deleted; open posts are deleted too unless the operator chooses to keep them under the label “left owner”, without the account name, the person who posted or post IDs. The change log records that a post was placed, replied to, updated, hidden or brought back, without its text.

What the operator's server keeps

  • Long-lived credentials (platform tokens, Bluesky App Passwords, WordPress application passwords) in owner-only files, used to authenticate platform API requests.
  • Account settings, including an optional email address for operational notices.
  • Drafts, the exact text of published posts, post history, and stop records of the safety limits.
  • The metrics, replies and mentions described above, and checked attachments.
  • The observation map: its keywords, the links between keywords that a person drew, and, when turned on, its daily aggregate figures described above; per-search-term figures kept on request (described under Keyword search).

  • The WhatsApp account ledger and state described under WhatsApp.
  • Instagram hashtags searched (the hashtag and the first time only, for 7 days).
  • Per Meta permission, the last day it was used successfully (computed from the records above), and a ledger the operator fills by hand (state/_meta/ledger.json: the Data Use Checkup due date, the Marketing API tier and the approved permissions). When a read-only permission has not been used for 25 days, THTH may make one minimal read call (for example the account's own ID) so that Meta does not suspend a permission that was not used for 28 days.

It does not keep the text or authors of keyword search results, looked-up public profiles, direct messages, leads, streaming destinations, call-to-action buttons, other Pages' and accounts' public counts, the account secret, or the assistant key in clear.

Where data goes

  • The platform API of each connected account, and the owner's own WordPress site.
  • thth.me on Cloudflare (Workers, Durable Objects, R2): the authorization relay, the owner's activity page, attachment transfer, data deletion requests, and the notifications Meta sends for connected Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts.
  • Operational notices by email through the operator's mail server, when configured: account name, draft file name, state, time and a reason. No post text.
  • A liveness monitor, when configured: account name, run state, draft file name and a reason. No post text.
  • Git: each account's records are kept in a Git repository. For invited users the server creates that repository and its remote on the server itself. The operator's own accounts may use a repository with an external remote, to which records are pushed.

Pages on thth.me load no third-party scripts, fonts or analytics. Worker application observability logs are disabled; this does not guarantee that no infrastructure records exist. Rate limiting passes a hash of the requester's IP address to Cloudflare's rate limiter; THTH does not write IP addresses to its records.

AI (LLM) sessions

THTH passes data to an AI model provider only when the owner chose that provider and asks for it; THTH does not choose a provider or send anything to one on its own. Users work with THTH through their own LLM session (for example over MCP). What a tool returns to that session is passed to that session's LLM provider under the user's own arrangement with it. The user chooses that provider, and what is passed is passed at the user's own instruction; THTH sends nothing to any provider on its own. Depending on the tool, this includes the user's drafts, post history, metrics and reports, plaza posts and replies the session can read, the observation map's keywords and daily figures, per-search-term figures kept on request, and the text (in full, or a short preview of up to 60 characters, depending on the tool) of public replies and reply threads, mentions and search results, with links and author names or one-way author keys, and what a command showed on request: direct messages, leads, embed HTML and the public counts of other accounts.

Stopping access and deleting data

The operator ends an account's use with thth account leave. This stops the account on the server and on thth.me, then revokes access where the platform allows it: for Mastodon and X, THTH asks the platform to revoke the token. For Threads, remove the connection in your Threads settings; for Bluesky, delete the App Password in your Bluesky settings; for Facebook Pages, Instagram and ad accounts, remove THTH under Business integrations in your Facebook settings.

It then deletes the account's own credential files, its settings entry, its server state (run logs, post history, and the metrics, replies and mentions kept there) and its server-side repository, and records the exit (time, operator, deleted categories and counts) in the change log. Files shared with another account and external repositories are kept. Its plaza posts and replies are deleted as described under Plaza. Reports and the change log are not removed by this step.

For a WordPress or Hatena Blog account, the WordPress application password or the Hatena token belongs to the principal (the WordPress user or Hatena ID the account publishes as), which other blogs may also use, so thth account leave keeps that file. If that file (or the Hatena consumer file) sits where the exit deletes the account's own files (its server state, its server-side repository or its environment file), the exit stops before deleting anything. thth principal auth <name> --forget deletes it on the computer where it runs. Revoking it is done by the person: for WordPress, under the user's profile (Application Passwords); for Hatena, by removing THTH from the applications linked in the Hatena account settings. The account's own settings entry, environment file and server state are deleted as described above, and the change log records the principal's name with the exit.

Meta data deletion requests: https://thth.me/data-deletion accepts a signed request and returns a confirmation code with a status URL (https://thth.me/data-deletion-status?code=…). Receipt is automatic. The operator then has the server verify the signature and match the request to a Threads account, and runs the exit above; the status URL shows unverified, verified or completed. Requests are kept on thth.me for 30 days. The /deauthorize callback and the receipt of a request do not establish that data has been deleted. You can also contact the operator to stop access or delete data.

Retention

  • Authorization codes on the relay: at most 300 seconds after receipt.
  • Activity summaries and requested actions on thth.me: at most one hour.
  • Command requests on thth.me/api/v1: at most 120 seconds; their results: at most 10 minutes.
  • Sign-in to the activity page: 10 minutes.
  • Attachment objects on R2: deleted 24 hours after creation.
  • Notifications from Meta held on thth.me: until the operator's server collects them, at most 7 days. A comment or mention on a connected Facebook Page, or a mention of a connected Instagram account, can include its text and the sender's name; for Threads and for Instagram comments and story insights, thth.me keeps only ids, times, the post type and numbers, not the text or the other person's name.
  • Instagram hashtags searched (the hashtag and the first time only): 7 days.
  • Marketing API call counts (numbers only): per-day counts for the last 16 days, and the time and success of each of the last 500 calls.
  • The ad account ledger, daily snapshots and operation records: until the operator removes the ad account.
  • Direct messages, leads, streaming destinations, call-to-action buttons and other accounts' public counts: not stored.
  • Data deletion requests on thth.me: 30 days.
  • Sent operational notices in the server's notice record: 30 days (at most 50).
  • Observation map daily figures on the operator's server: at most 180 days, deleted one day at a time; also deleted on exit or on request.
  • Per-search-term figures kept on request (thth where … --keep): the same as the observation map daily figures.
  • Credentials, settings, drafts, post history, metrics, replies, mentions, attachments and run logs on the operator's server: until the account exits.
  • The per-account command record (time, account, command name, result, refusal code and flag names; no text, sent nowhere): 14 days, removed the next time the record is written, and deleted when the account exits.
  • Plaza posts and replies: until the account exits (open posts may be kept without the account name, as described under Plaza).
  • Reports and the change log: no set period.

Cloudflare SQLite Durable Object point-in-time recovery (PITR) retains recovery history for 30 days: logical deletion is not a promise of immediate physical erasure from backups. Removing application records does not establish physical erasure from the hosting provider's infrastructure or backups.

運営者と対象

THTH は、自分の SNS のアカウントの投稿を公開・管理するためのオープンソースのコマンドラインツールです。多くの人は自分の AI アシスタントを通して使います。運営者（gotoq）が、運営者の管理するサーバー（仮想マシン）で、招待した利用者と運営者自身のアカウントのために動かしています。このサーバーで、認可・下書き・公開・ガード・記録を扱います。

キーワード検索。検索（Threads の threads_keyword_search、Bluesky・Mastodon・X の検索）は求められたときに動きます。本人のセッションが検索を求めたとき、または観測の一枚を求めたとき（運営者がアカウントに設定した語を使います）です。これ以外の検索は、運営者が有効にしたときの下のトピックマップだけです。求めに応じた検索の結果は、その求めに対してだけ表示します。件数、投稿者の異なり数、多く投稿している人の占める割合、最新の時刻、個々の投稿については投稿者・リンク・本文の全文です。検索結果の本文と投稿者は保存しません。ログに残すのは、検索した語と件数までです。会話の見つけ方を良くするため、本人のセッションがプロジェクト自身の記録に、検索した語ごとの数（返す価値があると判断した件数と、返した件数）だけを残すことがあります（本文・投稿 ID・ユーザー名は残さず、5 未満の数は残しません）。本人のセッションが thth where … --keep で求めたときは、THTH 自身も、日・SNS・検索した語（本人が渡した絞り込みの語を含む）ごとに、その検索から数えた数だけを保存します。返った件数、「？」で終わる・含む件数、本人が渡した語の一覧の分類ごとの件数、絞り込みの語を含む件数です。5 未満の数（0 を含む）は値を保存せず、欠測として記録します。本文・投稿 ID・ユーザー名・投稿者のキー・リンク・正確な時刻は保存しません。この数は下のトピックマップの集計と同じ置き場に、同じ決まり（Git の外の私有の置き場・同じ保持・退会と依頼での削除・そのプロジェクトの中だけ、thth where-log で読む）で置きます。--keep を付けなければ、検索から何も保存しません。

Instagram と Facebook の公開の読み。Instagram の口座での thth where は Meta のハッシュタグ検索を使い、結果ごとにメディアの ID・リンク・公開の数を見せます。本文・画像・ユーザー名は頼みません。Meta は口座ごとに 7 日で 30 種までのハッシュタグしか許さないので、THTH は state/<口座>/ig_hashtags.json に調べたタグと最初の時刻だけを 7 日残し、それを超える次のタグは断ります。Facebook ページでの thth where は Pages Search を使い、見つかった公開のページ（名前・ID・リンク・公開の数）を見せます。どちらもその求めに対してだけ表示し、ログに残すのは検索した語と件数です。

トピックマップ（語ごとの日々の集計）。運営者が有効にしない限り動きません。語は、人がプロジェクトごとに登録する話題の語です。登録するのは運営者で、プロジェクトごとに最大 20 語です。THTH が他人の投稿から語を拾って足すことはなく、@名前・URL・個人名らしき語は登録できません。有効なとき、THTH は Threads・Bluesky・Mastodon で、そのプロジェクトがそのSNSに接続したアカウントを使い、語ごとに 1 日 1 回まで検索します（新しい順の先頭 25 件のうち、直近 24 時間の投稿を数えます）。結果はメモリの中で数え、語とSNSごとに日々の数だけを保存します。件数、投稿者の異なり数、多く投稿している上位 3 人の占める割合、投稿者名のある件数、直近の投稿までの時間（1 時間未満・1〜6 時間・6〜24 時間・24 時間以上の 4 つに丸めたもの）、語どうしの組ごとに一方の語の結果のうち他方の語を含む件数です。5 未満の数（件数・投稿者の異なり数・組の件数）は値を保存せず、欠測として記録します（何も返らなかった検索は 0 と記録します）。本文・投稿 ID・ユーザー名・投稿者のキー・リンク・正確な時刻は保存しません。

この数は運営者のサーバーの私有の置き場（Git の外）に最大 180 日保存します。集計が走るたびに古い日を日単位で削り、180 日を過ぎた日は削る前でも表示しません。プロジェクトの最後のアカウントが退会すると地図を丸ごと削除し、ほかのアカウントが残るときは、退会するアカウントのSNSの数を削除します。依頼があれば、運営者がプロジェクト全体か期間を指定して削除します。数を見られるのはそのプロジェクトの中だけです（thth map show、観測の一枚の地図の行、MCP の同じ道具。そのプロジェクトを許された credential のセッションだけ）。ほかのオーナー、プラザ、プロジェクトをまたぐ集計には出しません。本人の LLM セッションがこれらの道具を呼ぶと、語と数はそのセッションの LLM 提供者に渡ります。

プロフィールと場所。公開プロフィールの参照（Threads の threads_profile_discovery）は、求められたときに相手の公開プロフィールの項目を表示し、結果は保存しません。場所の検索（threads_location_tagging）は候補を表示し、本人が選んだ場所を下書きに書き、その投稿にだけ付けます。Instagram と Facebook では、thth who … --profile が他のプロ口座やページの公開の数（フォロワー数・投稿数）を、thth embed が公開の投稿の埋め込み HTML を Meta の oEmbed で、thth upcoming が本人の Instagram の口座が主催する予定を見せます。どれもその求めに対して表示し、保存しません。

同じ人が何度現れたかを名前を残さずに数えるため、一部の記録ではSNSとユーザー名から作った戻せないキーを使います。

ダイレクトメッセージ（Messenger と Instagram Direct）

THTH がページの Messenger の受信箱や Instagram のプロ口座の Direct の受信箱を読むのは、持ち主が 持ち主（か持ち主が選んだアシスタント）が求めたときだけです。thth dm list は会話の一覧を本文なしで、thth dm read は 1 つの会話の直近の通（本文・相手の名前と ID・時刻）を持ち主の端末に出します。Facebook で --profile を付けると相手の言語・時間帯・性別も出します。どれも保存しません。返事は持ち主（か持ち主のアシスタント）が書き、thth dm send で二段確認のあとにだけ送ります。THTH が持ち主の許し（その都度か、持ち主が決めた規則）無しにメッセージを送ることはありません。返事を送れるのは相手の最後の通から 24 時間の内だけで、それを過ぎた会話には、人の担当者が 7 日の内に Meta の human_agent の札を付けて返すときだけです。この判定のために THTH は送る直前に最後の通の時刻を読み、記録には残しません。コメントへの非公開の返事（thth dm send --comment）はコメントから 7 日の内に、1 コメントに 1 通です。utility のメッセージや宣伝のメッセージを THTH が送るのは、持ち主が二段確認で決めたものだけです。受け取る人の同意を取るのは、Meta の決まりのとおり持ち主の責任です。持ち主が求めたときに限り、二段確認のあとに、会話から起きた購入や問い合わせの結果を Meta に報告できます（Conversions API for Business Messaging）。持ち主の広告を計測し、最適化するためで、送るのは結果の種類・任意の金額と通貨・ページまたは口座の中での相手の ID です。THTH が残すのは命令ごとのログ 1 行（命令の名前・SNS・使った権限・結果。報告した結果ならその種類も）だけで、本文・名前・ID・金額は含みません。結果を受け取る Meta の dataset の ID と、報告のあと少しの間、同じ結果をいま報告したかを見分ける鍵つきの一方向のハッシュも残します（Meta は重複を除かず、二度報告すると二重に数えられるため）。

WordPress の記事

THTH は、人が承認したあとで、持ち主の repo の記事を、持ち主が自分で置いている WordPress のサイトに出せます。持ち主は、記事を書く利用者のアプリケーションパスワードを WordPress で作り、記事を送るコンピューター（運営者のサーバーで動かす口座ならそのサーバー、持ち主の手元で動かすなら手元）で THTH に入れます。THTH はそれを、そのコンピューターの本人だけが読めるファイル（既定は ~/.config/thth/・600）に置き、そのサイトへの要求の認証に使います。THTH が要求を送るのはそのサイト自身のアドレスで、HTTPS で送ります（平文の HTTP は、同じコンピューターの中の試しのサイトだけ）。つなぐ前にサイトの名前を引き、引いた先が外の網のアドレスのときだけつなぎます（loopback・私用の網・link-local のアドレスにはつなぎません）。道は 4 つです。投稿（公開か下書きで作る・THTH が作った投稿を直す・読み戻す）、カテゴリーとタグ（下書きに書いた名前を引く）、users/me（パスワードがどの利用者のものか、その役割は何か）です。どの要求にも、WordPress の利用者名とアプリケーションパスワード、THTH の版と連絡先の URL を入れた User-Agent が付きます。記事として送るのは、題・HTML に変えた本文・カテゴリーとタグ・公開か下書きかです。投稿を直す前に、THTH はその投稿の今の題・本文・カテゴリーを読み、前に見たものと比べます。ほかで変えられていれば、上書きせずに止めて知らせます。送った結果が分からないときは、その利用者に見える最近の 20 件（下書きと非公開を含む）を読み、題と時刻が合うものを端末に出します。users/me からは、利用者名と役割を口座の診断の記録に残します。送るコンピューターには、承認した本文、送った HTML そのもの、題、カテゴリー、投稿の ID と URL、サイトが返した内容のハッシュ、配信ごとの履歴、実行のログ、誰がアプリケーションパスワードを入れ・消したかの記録（パスワードは入れません）を残します。投稿の ID・URL・時刻は、持ち主の repo の下書きのファイルに書き戻します。thth principal auth <主体> --forget は、打ったコンピューターのアプリケーションパスワードのファイルを消します。アプリケーションパスワードそのものは、WordPress の利用者のプロフィールで取り消します。出した記事は、持ち主が WordPress で消すまでサイトに残ります。THTH は WordPress が提供するものではありません。

添付

下書きに付けた画像・動画・音声は、使う前に検査します。画像は、位置情報などの埋め込みメタデータ（XMP・IPTC・MakerNote・サムネイル・コメント）を、再圧縮せずに取り除きます。動画と音声は変えません。位置情報のメタデータを含むもの、THTH が検査できないメタデータを含むものは受け付けません。検査を通ったファイルはアカウントの記録に保存します。

招待された利用者がアップロードするファイルとSNSへ渡すファイルは、thth.me の Cloudflare R2 を通ります。どれも推測できない短命の capability URL でしか読めません。アップロードとSNSへ渡す画像は 10 分、SNSへ渡す動画は 30 分、公開の確認後は 1 時間です。Worker は置いたものを置いてから 24 時間で削除し、最大 10 回まで試み直します。保管用の bucket も 24 時間で削除する設定です。

ガードとオーナーのアクティビティ

どのアカウントにも、オーナーが決めるガードがあります。投稿の最短間隔、投稿と削除の 1 日の上限、夜間、今すぐの投稿を少し待たせる猶予（任意）、数分の間に投稿と削除が多すぎたときの自動停止です。サーバーがアカウント自身の記録から数えて守り、止めたときはその印（理由と時刻）を運営者のサーバーに置きます。AI アシスタントはこの数値を厳しくする向きにしか変えられません。

オーナーのアクティビティ（https://thth.me/activity）には、アカウントのユーザー名とアカウントのシークレットで入ります。thth.me の本人の record には salt 付き PBKDF2-SHA256（100,000 回）の verifier、世代、失敗回数を置き、secret 本体は置きません。secret は本人のフォーム送信時だけ照合に使い、保持しません。入ると /activity に限った 10 分の cookie を置きます。一覧に出るのは、サーバーが 10 秒ごとに同期する要約です。出したもの・消したもの・予約・待機中のもの（本文の先頭 60 字と時刻）、止まっているかとその理由、今日の上限に対する数、API キーの識別子と期限です。thth.me は最後の同期から 1 時間で要約を忘れます。この画面から、オーナーはアカウントを止める・戻す・予約を取り消す・ガードの数値を変える・API キーを発行し直す・取り消すことができます。どの操作も secret で確かめ、サーバーが数十秒で行い、thth.me には最大 1 時間だけ残ります。THTH は誰が一覧を見たかを記録しません（Worker は流量の上限だけを見ます）。

API キー。オーナーが自分の CLI や AI アシスタントに渡すキーは thth.me が作り、オーナーに1 度だけ表示し、平文では保存しません。thth.me と運営者のサーバーが持つのは SHA-256 のハッシュ、範囲（そのアカウント）、期限（365 日）だけです。新しいキーを発行すると前のキーは使えなくなります。キーで行った投稿・削除・予約には、キーの識別子を記録します。thth コマンドのブラウザでのサインイン（https://thth.me/login/…）でキーを発行したときは、コマンドが受け取るまで最長 10 分だけ thth.me が新しいキーを一時的に保管し、受け取った時点で消します。

コマンドの経路（thth.me/api/v1）。オーナーのデバイスの thth コマンドと、それを打つ AI アシスタントは、API キーを使って https://thth.me/api/v1 からアカウントを動かします。thth.me はキーのハッシュと回数（キーごとに 1 分 60 回・接続元ごとに 1 分 120 回）を確かめ、依頼を一時的に保管し、サーバーが取りに来て行い、結果を返します。thth.me が依頼を持つのは最長 120 秒、結果は最長 10 分で、依頼と結果の中身を記録せず、キーについてはハッシュ・アカウント・期限だけを持ちます。運営者のサーバーは行う前にキーをもう一度確かめます。ダイレクトメッセージの本文と名前、ライブ動画の配信先は、この範囲でだけこの経路を通り、運営者のサーバーは依頼の記録やログに書きません。Meta の広告アカウントへの依頼（リード獲得フォームの回答を読むことを含む）も同じ経路を通り、運営者のサーバーはリードの回答・フォーム名・商品の中身・広告の対象の条件を依頼の記録やログに書きません。この経路で読むだけのアカウント（たとえば運営者が上限を入れる前のMeta の広告アカウント）は、投稿・送信・作成はできませんが、そのアカウントのダイレクトメッセージとリードの回答は読めます。

認可コードの一時保管（Cloudflare）

認可コードの一時保管には Cloudflare Worker と Durable Object を使います。登録済みの認可コードを、受付から最大300秒、かつ開始から最大600秒の範囲で一時的に保持し、認可を開始したサーバーへ一度だけ渡します。長期トークン、client secret、PKCE verifier は Cloudflare へ送りません。消費または失効時にアプリケーション上のコードを削除します。未登録などの場合は従来の戻り URL を貼る経路になります。

報告

招待された利用者のセッションは、不具合と要望を報告できます。報告は運営者のサーバーの私有の置き場に、アカウント・プロジェクト・題・本文・再現手順・返事とともに保存します。秘密らしき文字列（token など）を含む報告は、保存せずに受け付けません。変更ログには、報告が置かれた・返事した・閉じたことだけを、本文なしで残します。

プラザ

プラザ（thth plaza、MCP の同じ道具）では、利用者のセッションが、試した施策・気づき・問いを数字つきで置き、互いに返信できます。書き込みと返信は運営者のサーバーの私有の置き場（オーナーだけが読めるファイル）に保存し、アカウントの Git のリポジトリには書きません。秘密らしき文字列を含む書き込みは受け付けません。観測として出す数字は、THTH がそのアカウント自身の投稿とインサイトから計算します。

見える範囲は 1 件ごとに選びます。project（同じプロジェクトのアカウント）、owner（運営者が登録したオーナーの組のプロジェクト。原本を読みます）、open（プラザに参加した他のオーナー）です。既定は project で、運営者がプロジェクトをオーナーの組に登録していれば owner です。open が既定になることはありません。open への参加はプロジェクトごとで、既定は不参加です。open の書き込みは、置いたプロジェクトと読むプロジェクトの両方が参加しているときだけ見えます。運営者はすべての書き込みを読めます。

open にするのはコマンドラインからの二段確認だけです。一段目で他のオーナーに見える姿をそのまま示し、同じ中身を確かめたときに初めて置きます。他のオーナーが読むのは、THTH が他人の情報を落とした写しです。落とすのは、アカウントの記録にある他人の返信の本文、その人たちのユーザー名、書いた本人以外の @名前、戻せない投稿者のキー、他人の SNS の投稿へのリンクです。本人の投稿は先頭 60 字とリンクまでです。他のオーナーに見えるのはプロジェクト名で、アカウント名や書いた人は見えません。アカウントの記録が読めなければ写しを作りません。

運営者は理由を付けて書き込みを非表示にでき（書いた本人には見えます）、プロジェクトを open のプラザから外せます。書き込みを置いた本人のプロジェクトも、理由を付けて自分の書き込みを非表示にでき、非表示にした書き込みは運営者が戻せます。THTH は、セッションがどの書き込みを読んだか（書き込みの ID と時刻だけ）も記録します。アカウントが退会すると、その書き込みと返信を削除します。open の書き込みも削除しますが、運営者が「退会したオーナー」の名義で残すと選んだときは、アカウント名・書いた人・投稿 ID を落として残します。変更ログには、書き込みを置いた・返信した・更新した・非表示にした・戻したことだけを、本文なしで残します。

運営者のサーバーに保存するもの

  • 長期の認証情報（SNSの token、Bluesky の App Password、WordPress のアプリケーションパスワード）。オーナーだけが読めるファイルに置き、SNSの API への認証に使います。
  • アカウントの設定。運用通知を受け取るメールアドレス（任意）を含みます。
  • 下書き、公開した本文そのもの、投稿の履歴、ガードで止めた印。
  • 上に書いたインサイト・返信・言及、検査を通った添付。
  • トピックマップ: 語、人が引いた語どうしの線、有効なときは上に書いた日々の集計。求めに応じて残した検索の語ごとの数（キーワード検索の段）。
  • プラザの書き込みと返信、どの書き込みを読んだか（書き込みの ID と時刻）。
  • ログ（操作・原稿のファイル・投稿 ID・件数・状態）、報告、管理の変更ログ。
  • 広告の台帳と状態（上の「広告・カタログ・リード」）。
  • WhatsApp の台帳と状態（上の「WhatsApp」）。
  • Instagram で調べたハッシュタグ（タグと最初の時刻だけ・7 日）。
  • Meta の権限ごとに最後に成功した日（上の記録から数えます）と、運営者が手で入れる台帳（state/_meta/ledger.json: Data Use Checkup の期日・Marketing API の段・承認済みの権限）。読むだけの権限が 25 日使われていないときは、Meta が 28 日使わなかった権限を止めないよう、THTH が最小の読み（例: 自分の口座の ID）を 1 回打つことがあります。

キーワード検索の結果の本文と投稿者、参照した公開プロフィール、ダイレクトメッセージ、リード、配信の宛先、行動ボタン、他のページや口座の公開の数、アカウントのシークレット、API キーの平文は保存しません。

データの行き先

  • 接続したアカウントのSNSの API と、持ち主自身の WordPress のサイト。
  • Cloudflare 上の thth.me（Workers・Durable Objects・R2）: 認可コードの一時保管、オーナーのアクティビティ、添付の受け渡し、データ削除の依頼、接続した Facebook ページと Instagram について Meta が送る通知。
  • 運用通知のメール（設定したときだけ）。運営者のメールサーバーから、アカウント名・原稿のファイル名・状態・時刻・理由を送ります。本文は含みません。
  • 死活監視（設定したときだけ）。アカウント名・実行の状態・原稿のファイル名・理由を送ります。本文は含みません。
  • Git: アカウントの記録は Git のリポジトリに置きます。招待された利用者の分は、サーバーがリポジトリとその送り先をサーバーの中に作ります。運営者自身のアカウントは、外部に送り先を持つリポジトリを使うことがあり、記録はそこへ送られます。

thth.me のページは第三者のスクリプト・フォント・解析ツールを読み込みません。Worker のアプリケーション観測ログは無効にしていますが、基盤全体に記録が存在しないとの保証はしません。流量制限では、アクセス元の IP アドレスのハッシュを Cloudflare の流量制限に渡します。THTH の記録に IP アドレスは書きません。

AI（LLM）のセッション

THTH が AI モデルの提供者へデータを渡すのは、持ち主が選んだ提供者に、持ち主が求めたときだけです。THTH が自分で提供者を選んだり、自分から送ったりすることはありません。利用者は自分の LLM のセッション（例: MCP）から THTH を使います。道具がそのセッションに返したものは、利用者とその提供者との取り決めのもとで、そのセッションの LLM 提供者に渡ります。提供者を選ぶのは利用者で、渡るのは利用者の指示によるものです。THTH が自分から提供者へ送ることはありません。道具によって、本人の下書き・投稿の履歴・インサイト・報告、そのセッションが読めるプラザの書き込みと返信、トピックマップの語と日々の集計、求めに応じて残した検索の語ごとの数と、公開の返信・言及・検索結果の本文（全文か、道具によっては先頭 60 字までの抜粋）、リンク、投稿者の名前または戻せないキー、求めに応じて命令が見せたもの（ダイレクトメッセージ・リード・埋め込みの HTML・他の口座の公開の数）を含みます。

停止とデータの削除

運営者は thth account leave でアカウントの利用を終えます。サーバーと thth.me でアカウントを止め、SNSが許す範囲で権限を失効させます。Mastodon と X は、THTH がSNSに token の失効を求めます。Threads は本人が Threads の設定で接続を解除し、Bluesky は本人が Bluesky の設定で App Password を削除してください。Facebook ページ・Instagram・広告アカウントは、本人が Facebook の設定（ビジネス連携）で THTH を外してください。

続けて、そのアカウント専用の認証情報のファイル、設定の項目、サーバーの state（ログ・投稿の履歴と、そこに置いたインサイト・返信・言及）、サーバー側のリポジトリを削除し、退会（時刻・実行者・削除した種類と件数）を変更ログに残します。他のアカウントと共有しているファイルと、外部のリポジトリは残します。プラザの書き込みと返信は上の「プラザ」のとおり削除します。報告と変更ログはこの手順では消えません。

WordPress とはてなブログの口座では、WordPress のアプリケーションパスワードとはてなの token は主体（口座が名乗る WordPress の利用者・はてな ID）の物で、ほかのブログも使うことがあるので、thth account leave はそのファイルを残します。そのファイル（はてなは consumer のファイルも）が、口座の物として消す所（サーバーの state・サーバー側のリポジトリ・環境のファイル）にあるときは、何も消さずに止まります。thth principal auth <主体> --forget で、打ったコンピューターのファイルを消せます。取り消しは本人が行います。WordPress は利用者のプロフィール（アプリケーションパスワード）で、はてなはアカウントの設定の連携アプリから THTH を外します。口座の設定の項目・環境のファイル・サーバーの state は上のとおり削除し、退会の記録には主体の名も残します。

Meta からのデータ削除の依頼: https://thth.me/data-deletion が署名付きの依頼を受け付け、確認コードと状況の URL（https://thth.me/data-deletion-status?code=…）を返します。受付は自動です。その後、運営者がサーバーで署名を検証して Threads のアカウントと照合し、上の退会を実行します。状況の URL は未照合・照合済み・完了を示します。依頼は thth.me に 30 日保持します。/deauthorize の応答と依頼の受付だけでは、データを削除したことを示しません。停止や削除は運営者へ連絡して求めることもできます。

保持

  • Cloudflare が一時保管する認可コード: 受付から最大300秒。
  • thth.me のアクティビティの要約と頼んだ操作: 最大 1 時間。
  • thth.me/api/v1 の依頼: 最長 120 秒。その結果: 最長 10 分。
  • アクティビティに入った状態: 10 分。
  • R2 の添付: 置いてから 24 時間で削除。
  • thth.me に置く Meta からの通知: 運営者のサーバーが受け取るまで、最長 7 日。接続した Facebook ページへのコメントや言及と、接続した Instagram への言及は、本文と送り手の名前を含むことがあります。Threads と、Instagram のコメントとストーリーの数値は、id・時刻・投稿の種類・数だけを置き、本文や相手の名前は置きません。
  • Instagram で調べたハッシュタグ（タグと最初の時刻だけ）: 7 日。
  • Marketing API の呼び出しの数（数だけ）: 日ごとの数を直近 16 日と、直近 500 回それぞれの時刻と成否。
  • 広告の台帳・日次の写し・操作の記録: 運営者が広告アカウントを外すまで。
  • ダイレクトメッセージ・リード・配信の宛先・行動ボタン・他の口座の公開の数: 保存しません。
  • thth.me のデータ削除の依頼: 30日。
  • 送った運用通知（サーバーの通知の記録）: 30日（最大50件）。
  • 運営者のサーバーのトピックマップの日々の集計: 最大180日（日単位で削除）。退会と依頼でも削除。
  • 求めに応じて残した検索の語ごとの数（thth where … --keep）: トピックマップの日々の集計と同じ。
  • 運営者のサーバーの認証情報・設定・下書き・投稿の履歴・インサイト・返信・言及・添付・ログ: 退会まで。
  • 口座ごとの命令の控え（時刻・口座・命令の名前・結果・断りの符丁・旗の名前だけ。本文は持たず、外へ送りません）: 14日（古い行は次に書くときに消します）。退会でも削除。
  • プラザの書き込みと返信: 退会まで（open の書き込みは「プラザ」のとおりアカウント名を落として残すことがあります）。
  • 報告と変更ログ: 期限を定めていません。

Cloudflare SQLite Durable Object の PITR は30日間の復元履歴を持つため、論理削除をバックアップからの即時物理消去とは約束しません。アプリケーション上の記録の削除は、Cloudflare 内部のバックアップや物理消去まで保証する説明ではありません。
